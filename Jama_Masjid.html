OLD DELHI · MUGHAL MOSQUE

Welcome to Jama Masjid

One of the largest mosques in India, with a view over Old Delhi.

Insights

Jama Masjid was commissioned by Shah Jahan and built between 1650 and 1656. It is one of the largest mosques in India, and its vast courtyard can hold tens of thousands of worshippers.

The mosque is built of red sandstone and white marble. It has three great gateways, four corner towers, two tall minarets and three bulbous domes striped in black and white marble.

From the top of the southern minaret, visitors get a sweeping view over Old Delhi, the Red Fort and the market rooftops. The mosque is still an active place of worship.

Location

Jama Masjid is located on Meena Bazaar, Jama Masjid Road, Old Delhi – 110006, opposite the Red Fort.

BY METRO

The nearest station is Jama Masjid on the Violet Line. Chandni Chowk (Yellow Line) is also within reach by rickshaw.

NEARBY PLACES

Red Fort, Chandni Chowk, Karim's restaurant and Urdu Bazaar are all close by.

Useful Information

Dress modestly, with shoulders and legs covered, and remove your shoes before entering. Non-worshippers may not be allowed in during prayer times, especially on Fridays, so visit in the morning or afternoon. Check any camera fees and current timings at the gate.
