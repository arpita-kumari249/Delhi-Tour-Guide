THE STORY OF A CITY

History of Delhi

Delhi is a city built through centuries of history, shaped by different dynasties, cultures and empires.

ANCIENT PERIOD

Ancient Delhi

Tradition connects the area with Indraprastha, the city mentioned in the Mahabharata. The exact historical identification is debated, but the site is associated with Purana Qila.

12TH – 16TH CENTURY

Medieval Delhi

Delhi became a major centre of several Islamic dynasties, beginning with the Delhi Sultanate. This period left landmarks such as Qutub Minar, Tughlaqabad Fort and Quwwat-ul-Islam Mosque.

17TH – 19TH CENTURY

Mughal Delhi

Shah Jahan established Shahjahanabad in the 17th century, centred around the Red Fort and Jama Masjid. Chandni Chowk still reflects the heritage of this period.

1857 – 1911

British Rule

Delhi became a major centre of the Revolt of 1857. In 1911, the British announced the transfer of their capital from Calcutta to Delhi.

1931

New Delhi

Designed by Edwin Lutyens and Herbert Baker, New Delhi was inaugurated as the imperial capital in 1931. Its landmarks include India Gate and Rashtrapati Bhavan.

1947 – PRESENT

Independent India

After independence in 1947, Delhi became the capital of independent India. It expanded into a major political, cultural and commercial centre.
