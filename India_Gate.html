Welcome to India Gate

INSIGHTS

India Gate is one of the most recognizable landmarks of New Delhi. Standing at the heart of the city, this 42-metre-high sandstone arch was designed by Sir Edwin Lutyens and serves as a war memorial commemorating Indian soldiers who lost their lives during World War I and other conflicts.

The monument's grand arch and detailed inscriptions make it an important part of Delhi's history. The surrounding area is equally popular, with large green lawns and open spaces where visitors can relax and enjoy the atmosphere.

One of the best experiences at India Gate is seeing the monument after sunset, when it is illuminated and the surrounding fountains and gardens create a beautiful setting. The nearby National War Memorial can also be visited to learn more about India's military history and honour the country's armed forces.

India Gate is more than just a monument—it is a place where history, architecture and Delhi's lively outdoor culture come together. It is especially popular for evening walks, photography and enjoying the city's atmosphere.

LOCATION

India Gate is located on Kartavya Path in Central Delhi, close to important landmarks such as the National War Memorial, Rashtrapati Bhavan and Parliament House.

By Metro
The nearest metro stations include Central Secretariat on the Yellow and Violet Lines and Khan Market on the Violet Line. From these stations, visitors can continue by walking, auto-rickshaw or cab.

By Bus
Several Delhi buses operate through the central Delhi area. Bus routes connecting areas around Connaught Place, Central Secretariat and India Gate can be used to reach the monument.

By Auto or Cab
India Gate is well connected by road and can easily be reached by auto-rickshaw or cab from most parts of central Delhi.

Surroundings
The area around India Gate is surrounded by important national landmarks and spacious green areas. The National War Memorial is located nearby, while Kartavya Path leads towards Rashtrapati Bhavan.
India Gate is also relatively close to attractions such as Purana Qila, National Museum and Lodhi Garden, making it easy to include several places in a Central Delhi sightseeing trip.
