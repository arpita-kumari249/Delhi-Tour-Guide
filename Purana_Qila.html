Welcome to Purana Qila

Delhi's old fort, linked by tradition to the legendary Indraprastha.

OPENDaily
TIMINGSunrise – Sunset
ENTRYTicketed
NEAREST METROPragati Maidan

Insights

Purana Qila (the "Old Fort") stands on a mound that tradition connects with Indraprastha, the city of the Mahabharata. Archaeological digs here have found remains from many different periods, though the exact identification with Indraprastha is debated.

The present fort was begun by the Mughal emperor Humayun and completed by the Afghan ruler Sher Shah Suri, who took Delhi in 1540. It has massive walls and three main gateways: the Bara Darwaza, the Humayun Darwaza and the Talaqi Darwaza.

Inside are the Qila-i-Kuhna Mosque, built by Sher Shah in 1541, and the octagonal Sher Mandal, a two-storey tower in red sandstone. Humayun is said to have died after a fall from the Sher Mandal's staircase in 1556. An evening sound and light show tells the story of the fort.

Location

Purana Qila is located on Mathura Road, near Pragati Maidan, New Delhi – 110003, close to India Gate.

BY METRO

The nearest station is Pragati Maidan on the Blue Line. Supreme Court (Blue Line) is another option nearby.

NEARBY PLACES

India Gate, Delhi Zoo, Humayun's Tomb, Sunder Nursery and the Crafts Museum are all close by.

Useful Information

The fort is open daily in daylight hours. Early morning and late afternoon are the best times to walk its lawns and ramparts. Check current ticket prices, timings and the sound and light show schedule on the official ASI and Delhi Tourism websites.
