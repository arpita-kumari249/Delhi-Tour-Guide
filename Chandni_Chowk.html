Welcome to Chandni Chowk

Four centuries of bazaars, flavours and crowded, colourful lanes.

NEAREST METROChandni Chowk

Insights

Chandni Chowk was laid out in the 17th century as the main avenue of Shah Jahan's new city, Shahjahanabad. It is traditionally said to have been planned with the help of his daughter, Jahanara Begum, and runs from the Red Fort towards Fatehpuri Masjid.

Today it is one of the oldest and busiest markets in India. Lanes branch off in every direction: Dariba Kalan for silver and perfumes, Kinari Bazaar for wedding accessories, Chawri Bazar for paper and brass, and Khari Baoli, one of Asia's largest wholesale spice markets.

The area is also famous for street food. Look for Parathe Wali Gali for stuffed parathas, along with jalebis, chaat, kachori and lassi. Religious sites sit side by side here, including Gurudwara Sis Ganj Sahib, Gauri Shankar Temple, the Digambar Jain Temple and Fatehpuri Masjid.

Location

Chandni Chowk is located in Old Delhi – 110006, running west from the Red Fort.

BY METRO

The nearest station is Chandni Chowk on the Yellow Line. Lal Qila and Chawri Bazar are other stations nearby.

NEARBY PLACES

Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Khari Baoli spice market and Gurudwara Sis Ganj Sahib are all a short walk or rickshaw ride away.

Useful Information

Many shops open late morning and close in the evening, and a large part of the market is shut on Sundays. The lanes are very crowded, so keep your valuables secure and wear comfortable shoes. A cycle-rickshaw ride is an easy way to see the main streets. Choose busy, freshly cooked food stalls when eating on the street.
